Sign Posts on the Road to Success

Success isn't an accident. It begins the moment you can see what God wants you to see, and it's sustained by the willingness to work for it.

Main Text

“Beloved, I wish above all things that thou mayest prosper and be in health, even as thy soul prospereth.”
— 3 John 2, KJV

Key Points

1. Vision

Vision is insight into the plan of God for your life.

Every distinction begins with vision. Success is achieving God's purpose for your life — and that starts with constantly seeing pictures of a better tomorrow. Vision is a mental picture of a preferred, better future. If you can see what God wants you to see, you'll be where God wants you to be.

2. Diligence

Working hard with sense — there can't be success without work.

Work is how you create value. God blesses the work of your hand — but there has to be work for Him to bless. God's own principle is six days of work and one day of rest.

Important Quotes

  • “Vision is a mental picture of a preferred future. If you can see it, you can reach it.”
  • “There can't be success without work. Diligence is how you create value.”
  • “God blesses the work of your hand — so there has to be work.”

Practical Applications

  1. Write down one clear picture of what a better version of your next year looks like — not a wish, a specific picture.
  2. Audit your week: are there six days of real, valuable work, or is diligence missing from the rhythm?
  3. Trade one hour this week you'd normally spend idle for one hour spent building toward the vision you wrote down.

Prayer Points

  1. Ask God to open your eyes to His specific plan for your life — not a generic dream, but His picture.
  2. Pray for grace to work with diligence, not just activity, and for God to bless the work of your hands.
  3. Ask for the discipline to rest on the day God has appointed, trusting Him with the work undone.
