Catching God's Vision for Your Life

Discover why catching God's vision and plan for your life is essential for direction, focus, and living a fulfilled, un-wasted life.

Main Text

“The light of the body is the eye: if therefore thine eye be single, thy whole body shall be full of light. But if thine eye be evil, thy whole body shall be full of darkness. If therefore the light that is in thee be darkness, how great is that darkness!”
— Matthew 6:22-23, KJV

Key Points

1. The Three Planes of Vision

Man is a triune being—spirit, soul, and body—and must learn to use spiritual eyes across three dimensions.

We live life on three planes, and we must be able to use our eyes on those three dimensions. Man is a triune being: spirit, soul, and body[span_0](start_span)[span_0](end_span). Vision is insight into the plan of God for your life, allowing you to see with the eye of the spirit the things that are possible in Christ for you[span_1](start_span)[span_1](end_span).

2. Spiritual Insight vs. Surface Value

Just as crude oil is found deep underground, life-changing vision and purpose are discovered beneath the surface through the Spirit.

You cannot find crude oil on the surface; you must drill kilometers down before you can discover it[span_2](start_span)[span_2](end_span). Nothing that is life-changing is on the surface—it is in the depth[span_3](start_span)[span_3](end_span). The Holy Spirit searches all things, yea, the deep things of God concerning your life[span_4](start_span)[span_4](end_span).

3. A Reason for Living

Knowing God's plan for your life gives you a compelling reason for living, preventing you from wasting your time or following the crowd blindly.

Without vision, you will easily give up and follow the bandwagon of what everyone else is doing[span_5](start_span)[span_5](end_span). Knowing God's plan gives your life direction, focus, and a compelling reason for living so that you do not live a substandard or unfulfilled life[span_6](start_span)[span_6](end_span).

4. Your Unique Identity

God made you distinct with a unique destiny, so you should embrace your individuality rather than copying others.

You were made different to make a difference[span_7](start_span)[span_7](end_span). Don't try to be like other people; be comfortable in your skin[span_8](start_span)[span_8](end_span). Your vision and destiny are unique to yourself, and true fulfillment comes from running your own God-set race with patience[span_9](start_span)[span_9](end_span).

Practical Applications

  1. Spend time in prayer and the Word to seek out God's specific plan and vision for your life[span_13](start_span)[span_13](end_span).
  2. Avoid blindly following societal trends or copying other people's career paths without divine conviction[span_14](start_span)[span_14](end_span).
  3. Commit your daily activities and long-term goals to aligning with God's ultimate purpose for you[span_15](start_span)[span_15](end_span).

Prayer Points

  1. Lord, open the eyes of my spirit to see the glorious plan You have prepared for my life[span_16](start_span)[span_16](end_span).
  2. Father, deliver me from living a wasted or unfocused life driven only by surface pursuits[span_17](start_span)[span_17](end_span).
  3. Grant me the grace, courage, and steadfastness to run my own unique race and fulfill Your divine will[span_18](start_span)[span_18](end_span).
